About Us

HERCOB is an Interdisciplinary Group with experience in the Aviation Industry.

Due to our training and experience, we know and understand the needs of all our Clients and the solutions for their full satisfaction; obtaining as a result the optimization of the Quality, Reliability and Security of the Operations and Services of your company at a lower cost.

We are an aeronautical consulting company that offers a wide range of services to companies dedicated to aeronautical maintenance, passenger and cargo air transportation, corporate aviation and all types of aviation activities in Latin America.

    Urgent or "just-in-time"

    Urgent or "just-in-time" charters are used for shipments that are so urgent that no other means of transportation can be used. When automotive parts are needed to keep the production line from grinding to a halt, or an aircraft part needs to be delivered to repair an aircraft that has broken down due to a technical problem, we can help. With our up-to-date information on the location of thousands of aircraft worldwide, we always know which aircraft is best suited for the job and can usually charter your cargo within an hour of confirmation. On-the-spot charters are typically used to transport the following types of cargo:

    Automotive parts | Oil and gas industry equipment | Aircraft parts | Aircraft parts | Medical equipment and supplies | Medical equipment and supplies | Marine Medical equipment and supplies | Maritime | Humanitarian relief goods | Medical equipment and supplies | Medical equipment and supplies | Maritime | Humanitarian aid goods

    Flight representation: one of our charter experts is able to assist you to make sure your flight runs smoothly.
    Aircraft selection: with access to 50,000 aircraft and 130 different aircraft types, we always provide the most suitable aircraft for your needs.
    Access to more airports: Get to a remote location or simply get closer to your final destination than a scheduled service would allow.
    Experience: Each of our charter brokers have received intensive training and have spent time on the tarmac, so they understand exactly what it takes to manage a charter flight from start to finish.
    Global coverage: no matter where you want to fly to or from, our international network of offices provides you with local knowledge. Cost-effectiveness: our buying power and reputation allows us to find you the best private jet charter prices, ensuring you always receive the most economical solution.
